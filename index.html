About us

Started in South Windsor. Built for future engineers anywhere.

Engineering connects many ways of thinking. Aerospace engineering explores flight and space, mechanical engineering develops machines, and civil engineering shapes the built environment. Electrical, software and environmental engineering connect power, information and sustainable systems.

We're building a community where you can explore these fields, exchange ideas, and learn through practical investigation. Our student engineering initiative brings engineering opportunities to students across Connecticut, while our learning pathways are built for future engineers anywhere.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is Matrix Engineers?

Matrix Engineers is a free, student-led engineering education initiative based in South Windsor, Connecticut. We help students explore engineering through hands-on activities, discussions, and career exploration, so future engineers can find the field that fits them.

Who can join Matrix Engineers?

Any student who is curious about engineering can join, including high school students with no prior experience. Students in South Windsor and across Connecticut are welcome, and students elsewhere are invited to express interest too.

What engineering fields are covered?

Six fields: aerospace, mechanical, civil, electrical, software, and environmental engineering. Each learning pathway connects core ideas with practical exercises and career pathways.

Is Matrix Engineers free?

Yes. It is completely free for students, and built to make engineering opportunities accessible to everyone.

Where is Matrix Engineers located?

South Windsor, Connecticut. It is rooted in the local community, while the learning pathways are designed to be useful to students anywhere.
